Drag the scene to look around; double-click to recentre. The sky, sea and scenery are rendered in Blender, and the sun position is computed live in your browser. The horizon (Point Grey, Vancouver Island, Bowen Island, the Sunshine Coast and the North Shore) is traced from the Copernicus 30 m surface model, trees included, allowing for the Earth's curvature and refraction, so the moment the sun drops behind it should be right to within a minute. The land is relit for the sun's true position, and the sky and the sun's colour follow the forecast aerosol haze.
